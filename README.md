# Leyes

> *Governing laws of cognitive alignment, taste, and ambiguity resolution for AI agents.*

## The 6 Lenses
1. **`Morals.md`** — Non-negotiable personal North Star and hard red lines.
2. **`Ethics.md`** — External impact, accessibility, and system stewardship.
3. **`Considerations.md`** — Real-world hardware, network, and maintenance gravity.
4. **`Love.md`** — Aesthetic taste, deep-module craft, and learning-first ergonomics.
5. **`Hate.md`** — Anti-patterns, bloat, and lazy AI habits to actively reject.
6. **`Grey.md`** — Tie-breaker protocol when two lenses collide in ambiguity.
